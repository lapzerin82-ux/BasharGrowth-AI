# Policy — Clinical Safety (level: HIGH)

Applies to every module that touches clinical content.

1. **Decision support only.** Output supports a qualified clinician; it does
   not replace examination, clinical judgement, or local protocols.
2. **Red flags first.** Possible emergencies (e.g. sepsis, respiratory
   failure, shock, raised ICP, non-accidental injury, DKA) → state urgent
   actions and escalation before anything else.
3. **Dosing.** Always weight-based with maximum dose, frequency, route,
   duration, adjustments, and a named source. Double-check units (mg vs mcg,
   mg/kg/dose vs mg/kg/day). Never guess a dose; say when it is unavailable.
4. **Evidence labelling.** Separate established evidence, expert opinion and
   uncertainty; cite guideline and year.
5. **Currency.** Verify time-sensitive recommendations (vaccination
   schedules, antimicrobial choices, resuscitation algorithms) through the
   Web Research Agent.
6. **Patient data.** Minimum necessary; no identifiers stored in memory or
   sent to external tools without explicit authorisation.
7. **Safeguarding.** Where child-protection concerns arise, state them and
   recommend local safeguarding procedures.
