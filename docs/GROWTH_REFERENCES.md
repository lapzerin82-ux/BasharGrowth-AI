# Growth reference data and plotting method

## Bundled references

| Id | Chart | Ages | Measures | Centiles drawn | Source of LMS values |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cdc2000_infant` | CDC 2000, birth to 36 months | 0–36 mo | length-for-age, weight-for-age | 3, 10, 25, 50, 75, 90, 97 | CDC `lenageinf`/`wtageinf`, from CDC's own `cdcanthro` package (github.com/CDC-DNPAO/CDCAnthro) |
| `cdc2000_child` | CDC 2000, 2 to 20 years | 2–20 y | stature-for-age, weight-for-age | 3, 10, 25, 50, 75, 90, 97 | CDC `statage`/`wtage` (via `rcpchgrowth`) |
| `who2006` | WHO Child Growth Standards | 0–5 y | length/height-for-age, weight-for-age | 3, 15, 50, 85, 97 | WHO daily LMS tables (via `rcpchgrowth`) |
| `who2007` | WHO Growth Reference | 5–19 y (weight 5–10 y) | height-for-age, weight-for-age | 3, 15, 50, 85, 97 | WHO 2007 LMS tables (via `rcpchgrowth`) |

The CDC set reproduces the layout of the CDC 2000 "Set 2" clinical charts
(3rd–97th percentiles), which is the chart set supplied as the visual reference.
WHO charts are provided as an alternative (Settings → Default growth reference).

The JSON files in `core/src/main/resources/growthref/` are generated by
`tools/convert_growth_data.py`; no value is digitised from a chart image.
Unit tests (`core/src/test`) check published LMS values, e.g. CDC boys
stature at 24.0 months L = 0.941523967, M = 86.45220101, S = 0.040321528.

## How a point is plotted

1. `days = measurement date − date of birth` (exact calendar days).
2. `age (months) = days / 30.4375` (= 365.25/12, the WHO/CDC convention). No rounding.
3. The red × is drawn at x = that age, y = the measured value exactly as entered.
4. Centile curves: for each age, L, M, S are linearly interpolated between the
   tabulated ages, and the curve value is `M·(1 + L·S·z)^(1/L)` (or `M·e^(S·z)` when L = 0),
   with z the normal quantile of the centile (e.g. 97th → z = 1.8808).
5. z-score of a measurement: `((X/M)^L − 1)/(L·S)`; percentile = Φ(z).
   For WHO weight-for-age, WHO's restricted method beyond ±3 SD is applied.

Example (from the specification): DOB 15/03/2018, measured 24/09/2026 → 3115 days
= 8 y 6 m 9 d = 102.340 months; 121.7 cm is plotted at x = 102.340 months,
not at 8 or 8.5 years.

## Chart selection

Default chart per age (can be changed on the chart screen, and the choice is saved per patient):

* CDC family: birth–36-month charts below 24 months, 2–20-year charts from 24 months
  (length is measured recumbent < 24 months, stature standing ≥ 24 months).
* WHO family: WHO 2006 below 60 months, WHO 2007 from 60 months.

Measurements outside the selected chart's age range are not drawn; the screen
states how many are hidden. The PDF includes every chart of the family that
contains measurements.

## Mid-parental height

Boys (father + mother + 13)/2; girls (father + mother − 13)/2; target range ± 8.5 cm
(Tanner). MPH and its target range are drawn at the adult end (20 y) of the
CDC stature chart.
